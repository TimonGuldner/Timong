FAQ

Everything you should know before ordering.

What happens after I pay?

Stripe confirms the order, then we send a short intake email. Reply with your current proposal and the requested company-specific information. Production starts once the intake is complete and the proposal is readable.

Do I need to get on a call?

No. Roofing Proposal Co. is intentionally email-first and fully asynchronous.

How fast is delivery?

Our target is within 24 hours after complete intake and a readable proposal are received. If critical information is missing, we ask for clarification first. The 24-hour window is a service target, not a guaranteed deadline.

Can I remove homeowner information before sending the proposal?

Yes — and we recommend it whenever practical. We usually need the proposal structure and company-specific content, not homeowner personal information.

Will you change prices, warranty, financing or legal terms?

No. We do not invent or alter pricing, warranties, financing, legal terms, manufacturer relationships, certifications or other commercial facts.

What files do I receive?

The rebuilt proposal is delivered as editable DOCX and customer-ready PDF, plus the Objection Response Pack, Proposal Follow-Up Pack and Staff Usage Guide.

Is this proposal software or a subscription?

No. It is a one-time custom document and sales-material rebuild.

What if I want a change after delivery?

One revision is included within the scope of the original order.

Do you guarantee more closed jobs?

No. We improve clarity and professionalism of the materials but do not guarantee sales outcomes.

Who handles the payment?

Checkout is processed by Stripe. Roofing Proposal Co. does not store your full payment card details.
